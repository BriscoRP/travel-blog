# Project Vision

## Purpose

Project Atlas is a long-term AI-assisted travel publishing platform created and maintained by Rik Powell.

Its primary purpose is to preserve and share the real journeys, experiences and memories of the grandparents (M & G) through a fast, accessible and trustworthy travel website.

Every place published has been personally visited. The website is built from genuine notes, photographs and experiences provided by the family, with AI assisting in organising, validating and presenting the information while Rik retains full editorial control.

The website is designed to inspire other people to discover new places while creating a lasting digital archive for future generations.

Project Atlas is also a practical demonstration of how modern AI can be used responsibly to design, build, manage and maintain a professional website using simple, long-term technology.

If the website eventually generates enough traffic to produce advertising revenue, this is considered a welcome outcome rather than the primary objective.

The success of Project Atlas will be measured not only by visitor numbers, but by how enjoyable it remains to maintain, how useful it is to visitors, and how well it preserves family memories for many years to come.

---

## Design Philosophy

Project Atlas is designed around one simple principle:

**The publishing workflow is more important than the website itself.**

If publishing becomes difficult, the website will eventually stop growing.

Every technical decision should therefore make publishing simpler, safer and more enjoyable while maintaining a consistently high standard of quality.

---

## Core Principles

- Every place has been personally visited.
- Truth before traffic.
- Visitors always come before search engines.
- Simplicity over complexity.
- Performance is a feature.
- Privacy is the default.
- Accessibility is mandatory.
- AI assists but never invents facts.
- Human approval is required before publication.
- Build once, maintain for years.
- The simplest correct solution should always be preferred.

---

## Non-goals

Project Atlas is not intended to become:

- a social network
- a review platform
- an online booking system
- a user-generated content website
- a complex CMS

Its purpose is to remain a simple, trustworthy publishing platform for genuine family travel experiences.
