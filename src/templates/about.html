About Project Atlas

A long-term family travel archive built around genuine journeys, careful storytelling and human editorial control.

Preserving genuine journeys.

Project Atlas exists to preserve and share the real journeys, experiences and memories of our family through a fast, accessible and trustworthy website.

Every Place that eventually appears in the collection will have been personally visited. The aim is to create a useful public resource while keeping a lasting digital archive for future generations.

A family travel archive.

The stories begin with family notes, photographs and first-hand observations. Private originals stay in a private editorial archive; only material that has been reviewed and approved belongs on the public website.

We keep personal identities general unless everyone involved has explicitly agreed to be named.

AI assists. People decide.

AI can help organise source material, prepare drafts and check consistency. It must not invent missing information or replace editorial judgement.

Rik retains responsibility for reviewing and approving every public guide, link and publishing asset.

What Project Atlas is not.

  • Not a booking service
  • Not a public review platform
  • Not a social network
  • Not a source of user-generated content
  • Not a replacement for official visitor information
