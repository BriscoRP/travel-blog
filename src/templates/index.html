What Project Atlas is

A lasting home for real journeys.

Project Atlas is a small, static family travel website built to preserve memories and share useful first-hand experience.

It is not a booking service or a review platform. When information changes frequently, we direct visitors to an approved authoritative source instead of presenting copied details as our own.

Our principles

Why you can trust Project Atlas.

  • Personally visitedEvery published Place begins with a genuine family visit.
  • No invented factsUncertain information is flagged for review, never guessed.
  • Human approvalRik reviews every exact content version before publication.
  • Dated observationsDetails that may change are tied to a Visit or verification date.
  • Privacy by defaultPrivate family evidence and identities stay outside the public website.
