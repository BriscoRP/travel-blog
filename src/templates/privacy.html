Trust and transparency

Privacy

Project Atlas is designed to share approved family travel stories while keeping private evidence and personal information out of the public website.

Information this website collects

This public website shell contains no forms, accounts or public editing tools. It does not ask visitors to submit personal information.

The hosting provider may process basic technical request information needed to deliver and protect the website. This statement will be reviewed against the final hosting configuration before production publication.

Cookies, analytics and external services

This website shell does not set cookies or include visitor analytics, advertising or tracking integrations.

If that behaviour changes, this page must be updated before the change is published.

Private family material

Raw photographs, recordings, messages, personal notes, transcripts and private documents belong in a private editorial archive. They are not part of the public website.

Only approved publishing content and privacy-reviewed derivative assets may appear publicly.

Links to other websites

Project Atlas may eventually link to approved authoritative websites for information that changes frequently. Those websites are responsible for their own privacy practices.

Privacy concerns

No public contact channel has been approved for this preview. Private contact details will not be exposed to provide one.
