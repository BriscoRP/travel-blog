Lantern Quay is not a real destination. This prototype concentrates on how Project Atlas might distinguish a family memory from practical observations that could help someone decide whether a place suits them.

## The fictional visit

The invented family route followed a broad promenade before reaching a cobbled approach to a small harbour. A sheltered seat provided a stopping point halfway through the fictional walk.

## Practical review focus

Parking, toilets and the change in surface are kept together in the facts panel. The review should consider whether these details are prominent enough without overwhelming the story.

## Fictional caution

Any equivalent production observation would require a genuine Visit date, careful wording and Rik's approval before publication.
