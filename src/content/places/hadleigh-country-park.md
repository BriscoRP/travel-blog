We went primarily to learn about hiring bikes and using the trails which were created for the mountain biking events at the London 2012 Olympics. But there is so much more, with great paths for walking, trim trails, play areas, including for remote controls, not to mention the ruins of the 13th century which dominated the stunning landscape over the Thames Estuary.

## Visiting advice

Good walking shoes and a sun hat and cream, as there are lots of open spaces.
